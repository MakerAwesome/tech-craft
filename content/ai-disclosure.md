# AI assistance

Some of what you read here is drafted with AI help. Rather than tagging individual posts, here is
the whole policy.

<!-- EDIT ME — this is a starting draft. It has to be true for you, or it is worse than saying
     nothing. Adjust every line until you would defend it in an interview. -->

- **What AI does:** research, outlining, copy-editing, and checking technical claims against
  vendor documentation.
- **What AI does not do:** the substance. What I tried, what broke, and what I concluded comes
  from work I actually did on my own machines.
- **Verification:** anything published as working has been run end to end. If I did not re-run it,
  the post says so.
- **Authorship:** no post is credited to an AI, and no post is published that I could not explain
  line by line to someone who asked.

If a specific post breaks these rules, that is a mistake worth pointing out — tell me and I will
fix it.
